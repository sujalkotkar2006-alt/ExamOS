## Interpretation and scope boundaries

- Website/AI-tool access appears in the problem context. Executable names alone cannot identify what is used inside an allowed browser. P1-02 must specify an authorized managed-browser integration and its coverage; the agent must not imply universal website visibility.
- Active-window information currently means the foreground executable. Collecting window titles, URLs, or additional content is a separate data-scope decision, with notice, minimization, and tests; do not enable it implicitly.
- The expected agent service can have a managed lifecycle while keeping capture visible and session-bound. Service availability must never imply permission to monitor.
- Reporting is derived from the background need for examination-focused reporting; classroom controls come from the explicit problem. Specific control types and performance targets in the roadmap are proposed product acceptance, not quoted hackathon requirements.
- macOS and cloud administration are optional and do not gate the initial Windows/Linux LAN product.
- Multi-monitor support is an enhancement to assess after the focused primary-screen experience; its absence must be clearly communicated.

## Change record contract

For each meaningful change record: backlog ID, `REQ-xx`/`EXT-xx` IDs, expected behavior, acceptance evidence, observed result, and remaining limits. Update this matrix when functionality or accepted coverage changes. A feature may satisfy several IDs; preserve IDs when reordering the roadmap.
